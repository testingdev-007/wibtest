3Prompts worth keeping

Take these into real Excel or Outlook CoPilot and adapt them to your own data or inbox.

Build a dashboard from this data with a chart of [metric] by [category], and call out the highest and lowest performers.
The template behind the "great" Excel prompt above — swap in your own metric and category.
Find the email from [name] about [topic] — I think it mentioned a deadline.
Naming a sender AND a topic together narrows a search fast, even with no exact keyword.
Summarise this thread into three bullet points: what was decided, what's still open, and who owns the next step.
Naming the exact shape of the summary gets something usable immediately, not a generic paragraph.
Compare [this month] to [last month] in this data and tell me the single biggest change.
Asking for one specific insight, not "analyse this", gets a direct, checkable answer.

5Finding old chats and files

CoPilot can search across your own history in Teams and Outlook — including things from months or years ago that would take forever to scroll back to manually.

CoPilot in Teams and Outlook can search across everything you have access to — chats, channels, and emails — going back as far as your history exists, not just what you can remember scrolling to. The trick is the same one from the whole exercise above: name something specific, not "that thing from before."

What did Priya say about the Q2 budget in our Teams chat back in March?
A person, a topic, and a rough time period — enough to find one specific message in months of chat history.
Find the pricing spreadsheet Mike shared with me last year — I think it was called something like "Q4 pricing."
Works even with a half-remembered file name — CoPilot searches content and context, not just exact titles.
Did anyone ever send me the signed contract for the Cornwall project? Check both my email and Teams.
CoPilot can search across BOTH Outlook and Teams in one go — useful when you're not sure which one something landed in.
Summarise every decision made in the #onboarding channel before the summer, even the older messages.
Turns a channel nobody's opened in months into a quick, readable catch-up — without you scrolling through all of it yourself.
Has this exact question come up before in any of my chats or emails? If so, what was the answer?
Genuinely useful before asking a colleague something twice — CoPilot checks your own history first.
